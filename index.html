TELEMETRY REFERENCE // METRICS & FORMULA

What Each Telemetry Stat & Grade Term Means

◆ DAILY OPERATIONAL YIELD & UNCAPPED EXTRA POINTS FORMULA

Daily Score: BaseStudy(50) + BaseSteps(30) + NetExtraPts (Uncapped) + Verified Discipline(20)
• Uncapped 1.0× Extra Points When Satisfied: Raw extra points above 100% quota are 50×max(0, StudyRatio − 1) + 30×max(0, StepsRatio − 1) with zero cap and zero diminishing returns when both quotas are met, balanced (|StudyRatio − StepsRatio| ≤ 0.25), and penalty-free.
• Imbalance & Penalty Deduction on Extra Points: If one module is neglected (`<100%`), or over-performance is lopsided (`|StudyRatio − StepsRatio| > 0.25`), or active debt/overdue penalties exist, extra points are penalized proportionally.
How the 20-Point Verified Discipline Term is Calculated:

  • 1. Dual-Quota Synergy (0–8 pts): Multiplicative balance 8 × min(1, StudyRatio) × min(1, StepsRatio).
  • 2. 7-Day Consistency & Streak (0–7 pts): Up to 3.5 pts for low 7-day volatility (σ) once ≥3 days are logged, plus +0.5 pt per consecutive dual-quota clear day (up to 3.5 pts).
  • 3. Zero-Debt & Deadline Integrity (0–5 pts minus Penalty): Scaled by overall quota completion, minus verified deductions for active spillover debt, overdue tasks, or missing a module (-6 pts if Study or Steps is 0).
1. DEBT-TO-INCOME (DTI / 49H)

Current accumulated study deficit divided by 49h weekly capacity. <0.15 is Optimal; >0.35 is Critical Deficit requiring immediate amortization.

2. MAX DRAWDOWN & MTTR

Max Drawdown: Largest peak-to-trough study debt ever reached. MTTR: Mean Time To Recovery (days needed to pay back 1h of deficit).

3. VALUE AT RISK (VaR QUOTA RUIN)

Statistical probability (0–100%) of falling short of the 45h weekly base quota based on your 30-day mean and standard deviation.

4. OUTPUT VOLATILITY (14D & 7D σ)

Standard deviation of daily study hours or steps. Lower σ means machine-like daily consistency; high σ signals cram/crash swings.

5. KINETIC YIELD (STEPS / H)

Ratio of daily steps per study hour. Target zone is 1,200–1,500 steps/h. Dropping below 800 steps/h flags a sedentary lock warning.

6. ACTIVE-DAY MULTIPLIER

Compares your next-day study output after hitting ≥10,000 steps vs. after sedentary (<10k step) days.

7. TARGET HIT RATE & CLEAR TIME

Percentage of logged days that met or exceeded base quota, plus projected days to halve or clear any active debt at your 7-day pace.

8. QUARTILES (P25 / P50 / P75 / IQR) & r

P25: Floor day. P50: True median day. P75: Peak day. IQR: Middle 50% spread. r: Pearson correlation between steps & study.
